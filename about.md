# Project Overview: Safarino Travel Platform

## Introduction
Safarino is a comprehensive travel booking platform designed to simplify the process of planning and booking trips. The goal of this project is to provide users with a seamless experience for finding the best travel options, including flights and hotels.

## Objectives
- To provide an intuitive user interface for travel searches.
- To integrate various booking services into a single platform.
- To ensure a secure and fast booking process for users.

## Target Audience
The platform targets modern travelers who seek efficiency and reliability in their travel arrangements.
